Need To Know

For the week of March 9th – March 13th, each class will have a collection container in the front lobby. Classes will compete to raise the highest average amount per student. Students can also reach out to relatives who don't live nearby with our online payments. Additionally, there will be daily prizes, raffle baskets and school prizes! Rules & FAQ

Everything you need to play along.

It is our spring fundraiser! This fundraiser is in lieu of sending students home with the task of selling door-to-door & delivering products where you only get to keep a small portion of the sales. Please support your students by encouraging them to participate!
This game is simple. It is basically a competition to see which class can raise the most money. Since class sizes vary, the classes will compete to get the highest average amount per student. Each day of the week has a daily prize for the class that raises the most that day, and overall prizes for the top three classes.
Last year, HSO funded over $3,200 on school field trips, fulfilled more than $3,800 of teacher funding requests, and even organized 6 awesome events. Through our Coin Wars fundraiser, we helped to beautify the school and support classroom needs.
The week of March 9th - 13th, 2026.
Anyone can donate! Please ask friends, family, co-workers etc. to donate to your child.
You can donate by giving your child coins, bills, or checks (payable to Colwich HSO) that they can bring in any day for the week of March 9th-13th. They will put it into their class's container each morning. You can also donate online.
There are prizes for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place classes. 1st place: The winning class will enjoy a Pizza Ranch trip and a dress down day! The winning teacher receives a $100 Amazon card for the classroom.
