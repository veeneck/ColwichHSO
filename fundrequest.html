Teacher Request For Funds

We've Got You Covered 🏦

Teachers, HSO would love to be able to fund your request for field trips, classroom projects, or other financial needs. Please read the Fund Request FAQ, and then submit the form below!

Fund Request FAQ

Before you submit.

We're thrilled to support field trips, special events, or items that benefit the whole school! Each request is different, and approval will reflect HSO's operating budget for the given year. The most common types of requests are: Field Trips (1–2 per grade are easiest to approve, covering admission or mileage; parents may need to chip in for meals or treats), Classroom / Office Supplies, and occasionally parties for classrooms that do not take field trips. Every request is unique and reviewed case by case. If we can't approve something, we'll let you know and suggest ways parents might help cover the difference.
We don't have a set dollar amount per teacher because our budget varies each year based on fundraising and the number of requests. But don't let that stop you! Share your ideas, and we'll work to fund them fully, partially, or help you find other resources. Please note: Any single or cumulative classroom request over $500 will require a vote from all HSO members, and should aim to benefit the entire school.
Please submit your request in advance of our next quarterly HSO meeting so we can review it together. We understand last-minute needs happen, but early submissions—especially for larger requests—give us the best shot at helping you out!
Submit your request using the form above. You'll hear back within a week about the status of your request. HSO officers will vote on your request at the next quarterly meeting or fast-track it if needed. Requests over $500 need a vote from all HSO members. If a vote was not possible, or if more information is needed, your request will be discussed at the next quarterly HSO meeting. We encourage you to attend to share your idea! Please contact us or reach out to an officer if you have not heard anything regarding your request.
For larger requests, we love ideas that benefit the whole school! Think big—past projects include bleachers, furniture for The Nest, and our school beautification fund. These long-lasting improvements are fueled by Coin Wars, our biggest fundraiser, where we set goals for major items. Got a grand idea? Share it with us and consider pitching it at an HSO meeting!
